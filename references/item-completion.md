# Item Completion

Before replacing an item, run `scripts/inspect-item-opportunities.js` against
the validated baseline. Check empty augment sockets, quality, bonding,
corruption/enchant restrictions, and simple legal item-local improvements first.

Use the active runtime's `Data/ModRunes.lua` for legality. Test a material legal
augment as a small variant before replacement; compare representative lower and
best available tiers, then let $estimate-poe2-build-costs determine market
affordability. After measurement, price every legal item-local completion,
replacement, and craft candidate that may be reported, including premium or
dream-tier options. Pass minimum functional requirements separately from
optional rolls. Compare direct purchase and crafting, plus only acquisition
alternatives verified for the exact item and current game version. Use validated
baseline deficits, never stale saved configuration. Do not invent an enchant or
acquisition path or apply a permanent change without approval.
