# Item Completion

Before replacement, run `scripts/inspect-item-opportunities.js` against the
validated baseline. Check empty augment sockets, quality, bonding, and
corruption/enchant restrictions first.

Use the active runtime's `Data/ModRunes.lua` for legality. Measure representative
lower and premium legal completion tiers before market research. Then place all
surfaced completions, replacements, and crafts into one
$estimate-poe2-build-costs batch. Include dream-tier options, preserve candidate
IDs, and pass minimum functional requirements separately from optional rolls.
Do not price fully owned or configuration-only changes.

Compare direct purchase and crafting only through acquisition paths verified for
the exact item and current version. Use validated baseline deficits, never stale
saved configuration. Do not invent an enchant or acquisition path or apply a
permanent change without approval.
